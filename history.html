Library History & Foundations

Background

Bugema University Library has been the heartbeat of the institution since its inception in 1948. From humble beginnings with a handful of theological books, it has grown into a modern academic library housing thousands of physical and digital resources.

Vision

To be a premier global center of information excellence that inspires research, innovation, and holistic learning.

Mission

To empower the university community by providing seamless access to high-quality information resources, expert guidance, and a conducive environment for scholarship and service.
