Policies & Guidelines

General Rules

  • Silence must be maintained in all reading and research zones.
  • Food and drinks (except bottled water) are not permitted inside the library.
  • All users must scan their library cards upon entry and exit.

Borrowing Guidelines

  • Students may borrow up to 3 books for a period of 14 days.
  • Faculty members may borrow up to 10 books for a semester.
  • Reference materials and periodicals are for library use only.

Digital Policy

  • Internet access is intended for academic and research purposes only.
  • Users are responsible for the physical safety of library computers while in use.
